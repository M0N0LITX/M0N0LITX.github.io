04 / Security notes

Build carefully.
Share responsibly.

High-level security lessons can demonstrate your mindset without exposing private material.

⬡

Security is a habit,
not just a tool.

A place to collect general principles, reflections and practical reminders. Keep examples anonymous and avoid operational details from private environments.

01

Protect credentials

Use strong, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication. Never publish secrets in a repository.

02

Apply least privilege

Give accounts and applications only the permissions they genuinely need.

03

Think before sharing

Remove identifying details from screenshots, logs and examples before publishing.
